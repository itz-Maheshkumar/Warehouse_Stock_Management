📦 Warehouse Stock Management

# overview

why this exists, and what it actually does

problem.md

A regional spare-parts network can't answer the question that actually matters: will this SKU run out before the next truck arrives? A stock table shows today's count, not tomorrow's shortage — and for a CRITICAL part like a track roller or a hydraulic pump seal, a stockout doesn't just annoy a dealer, it stops equipment.

Nobody wants to reorder blind, and nobody wants a warehouse in Pune sitting on surplus while Chennai runs dry.

solution.md

CatParts India ties the whole chain together: parts and inventory across four regional hubs, dealer order intake, a demand forecaster, a stockout-risk scorer, and a recommendation engine that turns a risk score into a plain instruction — REORDER, TRANSFER or HOLD.

Every stock movement — a supplier receipt, a shipment, a transfer — is written to an append-only inventory ledger, so the number on screen is always explainable.

# order_to_action_lifecycle

what happens between a dealer's order and a warehouse's next move

or click any step

Dealer places an order

A dealer order carries SKU, quantity, region and dealer name. Its priority — NORMAL or CRITICAL — is driven by the part's own criticality rating rather than typed in by hand.

Inventory is resolved

The fulfilling warehouse's Available figure — on hand minus reserved, plus incoming — is looked up per SKU per hub, across the four regional distribution centers.

Demand forecast runs

The Demand Forecast Service applies a WEIGHTED_MOVING_AVERAGE over historical demand to project 7, 30 and 90-day figures per part, rendered as a historical-vs-forecast line chart on the part's detail page.

Stockout risk is scored

Current stock, reserved stock, incoming stock, forecast demand, supplier lead time and part criticality combine into a 0–100% risk score, bucketed Low / Medium / High / Critical.

A recommendation is issued

The Recommendation Engine turns the score into an action — REORDER from the supplier, TRANSFER from a healthier hub, or HOLD — each with a plain-language reason, e.g. "estimated transit duration is faster than supplier lead time."

A manager approves or rejects

Every recommendation card carries Approve / Reject buttons. Approving a reorder or shipping a dealer order moves its status forward — Pending → Approved or Partially Fulfilled → Fulfilled.

The ledger logs it

Every movement — a supplier RECEIPT, a dealer SHIP, a hub-to-hub TRANSFER — is appended to an immutable Inventory Ledger with a timestamp, reference and note, so the current stock figure is always reconstructable.

# what_it_does

the feature set, honestly summarised

📊

Executive dashboard

Live KPI strip — total parts, inventory value, critical/high-risk counts, open orders, pending recommendations — plus an inventory-health donut and a regional value chart.

📦

Multi-warehouse inventory

On Hand / Reserved / Incoming / Available tracked per SKU across four India regional hubs — Chennai, Pune, Delhi NCR and Bengaluru.

📈

Demand forecasting

A weighted-moving-average model projects 7, 30 and 90-day demand per part from historical order history, charted against actuals.

⚠️

Stockout risk engine

Stock, reserved, incoming, forecast demand, supplier lead time and part criticality combine into a 0–100% risk score with Low/Medium/High/Critical bucketing.

🧭

Smart recommendations

Auto-generated REORDER / TRANSFER / HOLD actions with a plain-language rationale, plus an Approve/Reject workflow and live status.

🚚

Dealer order lifecycle

Orders move Pending → Partially Fulfilled → Fulfilled, with a Ship Stock action that decrements inventory and logs the movement.

🧾

Immutable inventory ledger

Every receipt, shipment and transfer is appended to an audit trail — date, warehouse, type, quantity, reference, notes — never edited in place.

🏭

Regional distribution network

Per-hub cards show location, inventory valuation in ₹ and parts tracked, with drill-down into hub-level inventory.

🔐

Role-based admin

Superuser sign-in for the warehouse manager role, backed by Django admin, with a global parts/SKU search across the app.

# engineering_notes

decisions worth defending in an interview

An immutable ledger, not a mutable counter

Stock isn't just a number that gets overwritten. Every RECEIPT, SHIP and TRANSFER is appended with a timestamp, reference and note, so "why is stock 170?" always has an answer in the Immutable Inventory Ledger History.

Risk blends five signals, not one

Current stock alone doesn't decide risk — reserved and incoming stock, forecast demand, supplier lead time and part criticality all feed the same score, so a CRITICAL part with a 25-day lead time outranks a MEDIUM part sitting at the same stock count.

Forecast and risk are shown, not hidden

The demand chart and the risk score sit right on the part detail page next to the recommendation — not behind an opaque "risk: high" label with no explanation.

Recommendations carry their reasoning

Each TRANSFER or REORDER card states source, destination, quantity and why — e.g. "estimated transit duration (5 days) is faster than supplier lead time (25 days)" — not just the verb.

Four independent regional hubs

Chennai, Pune, Delhi NCR and Bengaluru each carry their own stock, so a national stockout risk can often be resolved locally with a TRANSFER before a supplier REORDER is ever needed.

Fixture-driven demo data

initial_data.json seeds realistic SKUs — engine filters, hydraulic seals, track roller assemblies — so the forecaster and risk engine have real history to work from immediately after loaddata.
